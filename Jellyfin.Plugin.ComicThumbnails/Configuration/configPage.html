Comic Thumbnails

Shows page thumbnails on the detail page of comics (.cbz, .cbr, .cb7, .cbt), below the people. Clicking a thumbnail opens the reader at that page.

The plugin adds its script to the web client's index.html while it is served; no files are changed. Turn this off if you load the script yourself (see README). Reload the browser after changing it.
The section is shown collapsed until you click its title.
Only this many thumbnails are loaded at once; use the arrows to page through large comics. 6 to 200.
80 to 800. Changing it creates new thumbnails; the old ones stay in the cache until you clear it.
Encoder quality from 1 to 100.

Cache

Thumbnails are stored in Jellyfin's cache folder under comic-thumbnails and created again when needed.
