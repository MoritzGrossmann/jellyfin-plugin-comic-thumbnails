# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Show empathy towards other people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam or other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed inappropriate.

To report a problem, open an [issue](https://github.com/MoritzGrossmann/jellyfin-plugin-comic-thumbnails/issues) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
